Line of credit

How it works

Draw funds up to an agreed available limit. Repayments and further access follow the facility terms, which may include review or renewal.

Repayments

Draw when needed; repayments still apply

What to consider

Available limits can be reviewed or reduced. Repeated drawings can build up commitments; minimum or scheduled repayments still apply.

What a provider will check

Financial and bank information to assess a credit limit, ability to repay and ongoing business performance.

Costs to compare

Interest or drawdown fees may apply, with possible facility charges. Check charges on used and unused funds.

How it differs

Some drawdown products are structured as multiple loans rather than a fully revolving facility.

A personal guarantee may be required for all unsecured loans, subject to the lender’s discretion. Finance is subject to lender underwriting and approval.
