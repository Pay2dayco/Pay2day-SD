Land and property projects

What we’ll ask

The funding amount and purpose, property value, existing borrowing, ownership and use. For bridging, we’ll also ask how and when you expect to repay.

Commercial enquiries only

We do not offer regulated residential mortgages or consumer buy-to-let. If you or your family live, or plan to live, at the property, or its status is unclear, we’ll first check whether the enquiry is within our service. No eligibility decision is made by this website.

Finance is subject to lender underwriting, valuation and approval. The secured property may be repossessed if repayments or other obligations are not maintained.
