Card-terminal funding

Funding collected from card takings through a compatible payment provider.

Explore funding

How it works

An agreed share of eligible card takings is collected through your payment provider before the remaining takings reach your bank account. Availability depends on your provider and agreement.

Repayments

Often repaid from card takings

What to consider

Compatibility matters. Check whether switching is needed, payment-processing fees and how funding affects terminal or merchant-service terms.

What a provider will check

Card-sales history and confirmation that the terminal or payment provider supports the proposed funding and collection setup.

Costs to compare

Funding cost plus any separate terminal and card-processing charges. Ask for each cost to be itemised.

How it differs

The collection method is the key difference here: deductions through your card-payment provider rather than bank-account Direct Debit. A terminal alone does not establish eligibility.
