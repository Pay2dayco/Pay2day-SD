Is Pay2Day a lender?

No. Pay2Day is a UK business finance broker. We introduce businesses to finance providers; we do not lend or provide financial advice.

Will exploring affect my credit score?

This explorer and the calculators do not run credit searches or send your answers to providers. A later finance application may involve business or personal credit checks. The provider’s process must be explained before you proceed.

What does “unsecured” mean?

An unsecured loan does not typically take a specific asset as security. A personal guarantee may be required for all unsecured loans, subject to the lender’s discretion.

Are the calculator figures a quote?

No. You enter the example rate, fees or funding cost yourself. The figures illustrate the maths and do not indicate an available offer, eligibility or affordability. Actual terms require a provider’s assessment.

What information will I need?

Have your business details, funding purpose, turnover, current commitments and relevant owner details available. Providers may ask for bank statements and other documents. The form lets you reuse information already entered in the explorer.

Can I submit or save the form here?

This version lets you preview the journey using example details. Online submission, secure saving, email confirmation and resume links are not connected yet. Call Pay2Day on 020 3397 8990 to discuss an application.
