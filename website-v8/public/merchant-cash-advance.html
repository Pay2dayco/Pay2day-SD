UNDERSTAND THE ROUTE

How it works

A percentage of eligible card sales is collected towards the advance and agreed cost. Collection amounts move with sales.

Repayments and costs

Often a fixed funding cost. Ask for total repayment, the collection percentage and any additional charges.

What to weigh up

Deductions reduce the card income you receive. Slower sales can lengthen the repayment period; check the agreement for any minimums or other obligations.

What a provider may need

Evidence of card takings, trading history, bank statements and a compatible collection arrangement. Provider assessment required.

How routes overlap

Card-terminal funding may be a way of arranging this same type of advance.

BEFORE YOU DECIDE

Look beyond the headline rate.

  • Total amount repayable and all fees
  • Payment frequency and term
  • Security and personal guarantees
  • Early repayment conditions
  • What happens if sales fall
