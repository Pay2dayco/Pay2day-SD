How this website uses browser technologies.

This version of the website

The Pay2Day pages do not set advertising or analytics cookies. We do not add third-party analytics, tracking pixels, embedded videos or review widgets. The Trustpilot box is a link; clicking it opens Trustpilot’s website, where its own policy applies.

Form and explorer entries

Entries remain in the open page’s memory. They are not stored in local storage or session storage and are cleared when you reload or close the page. Downloads are saved only when you choose to download them.

Hosting and security

The hosting platform may use essential technologies to provide access and security. Those are separate from the Pay2Day application. If non-essential analytics or advertising is added in future, this policy and the consent controls will need to be updated before those technologies are enabled.

Your browser settings

You can manage and delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking essential cookies may affect access to services that need them.
