BUSINESS FINANCE OPTIONS

Business Cash Advance

An advance against business sales, collected from your bank account by Direct Debit.

Explore funding

How it works

Receive an advance with an agreed funding cost. Repayments are collected by Direct Debit from your bank account, with any sales-based adjustments governed by the provider’s agreement.

Repayments

Direct Debit linked to business sales

What to consider

Check how the Direct Debit is calculated, whether it changes with sales, and any minimum collections. A sales-based agreement can take longer to repay when sales fall.

What a provider will check

Evidence of business sales, trading history, bank statements and the provider’s collection requirements.

Costs to compare

Often a fixed funding cost. Ask for total repayment, the collection percentage and any additional charges.

How it differs

Here, Business Cash Advance describes bank-account Direct Debit collections; card-terminal funding describes collections through a compatible payment provider. Product structures can overlap.

A personal guarantee may be required for all unsecured loans, subject to the lender’s discretion. Finance is subject to lender underwriting and approval.
